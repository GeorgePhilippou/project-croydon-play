MAKE YOURSELF AT HOME

Finding your way.

On iPad, tap an opening in the scene to move, and tap a pictured object to inspect it. Doorway labels are hidden; tap the opening itself. At the entrance, tap the right-hand passage to turn into the hallway. Tap the bottom edge of the room image to return through the door behind you.

On computer, use arrow keys or WASD to move, and click objects with the mouse. Clicking doorways also works. Tab/Enter and E inspection remain available for keyboard access. Found objects open a close-up with a little story. The hunt pauses while you inspect a non-target object and resumes when you close it. The 60-second timer continues while the map or controls are open.
